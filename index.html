How to play

Every day there's a new acronym. Guess the phrase it stands for.

  • Each word of your guess is checked against the answer.
  • Green — correct word, correct spot.
  • Yellow — that word appears elsewhere in the answer.
  • Grey — that word isn't in the answer.

You get 6 guesses. The word count is shown above the board — short connector words like "of" and "and" don't map to a letter, so it won't always match the acronym's length. A new puzzle drops every day at midnight.
